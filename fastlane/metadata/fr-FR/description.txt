Timebase is a calmer way to keep time across the world.

Add the cities you care about, scrub through the day to see what time it is everywhere at once, and plan meetings that actually land at humane hours. Watch your upcoming calendar events count down on the lock screen. Glance at every city from a widget on your home screen.

Designed for people who live and work across timezones — distributed teams, remote workers, families abroad, frequent travelers, and anyone who just wants to know what time it is somewhere else right now.

— FEATURES —

WORLD CLOCK
• Up to 8 cities, automatically ordered by timezone offset
• Time-of-day gradients that flow with the hour, anywhere on Earth
• Fluid parallax gradients that respond to how you hold your phone
• Scrub vertically through the day — every clock moves together
• Tap any city for daylight and the local date

MEETING PLANNER
• Pick participants by city, find a time that works for everyone
• Anchor the meeting to anyone's timezone — your home or theirs
• Save straight to your calendar with one tap

UP NEXT

WIDGETS (HOME & LOCK SCREEN)

LIVE ACTIVITIES + DYNAMIC ISLAND

SIRI + SHORTCUTS + SPOTLIGHT

CONTROL CENTER

ALSO

PRIVACY
Timebase does not collect, store, or sell your data. Cities, settings, and calendar access stay on your device or sync via your own iCloud account.
